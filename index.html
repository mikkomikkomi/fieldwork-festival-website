The festival

Two days of making, sharing and finding your next spark.

Explore hands-on workshops, conversations with creative founders and a marketplace of independent makers. Come curious and leave with new ideas and new connections.

Fieldwork Festival brings 400 independent designers, artists and creative founders into one yard for a weekend of practice: print a poster, bind a zine, paint a mural, hear how others built their studios — and sell or swap what you make at the Maker Market.

What's on

Three rooms, zero dead time.

Hands-on workshops

Poster printing, collage, zines and a community mural — small rooms, real materials, things you take home. Seats are first come, first served.

Talks on the Main Stage

Conversations with creative founders on building independent practices, partnerships that last, and finding your visual voice.

The Maker Market

Open 10–5 both days: independent makers selling prints, ceramics, clothing and paper goods. Bring a tote — you'll need it.

Open studios & the community mural

Saturday afternoon the workspaces throw their doors open, and on Sunday the whole festival paints one wall together. Add your layer before the closing conversation.

The place

Foundry Yard, Los Angeles

A raw industrial yard with room for a stage, a print studio, a mural wall and a market lane — close enough to downtown to reach by transit, big enough to leave your comfort zone in the coat check.

  • Doors 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM PT, both days

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What does my pass include?

Everything except food: all workshops, all talks, the Maker Market, open studios, the community meetup and the collaborative mural. Food stalls are on site both days and sold separately.

How do workshop seats work?

First come, first served. Studios are intentionally small, so if a session is must-do for you, hang around the Studio door a few minutes early. Talks on the Main Stage have plenty of room.

Can I see everything in one day?

Almost — but simultaneous sessions mean choices. Check the schedule: slots marked same time run in parallel across the Main Stage and the Studio. The weekend pass exists so you never have to mourn the one you missed.

What are the hours?

Doors at 10:00 AM and market closes at 5:00 PM, both Saturday December 5 and Sunday December 6, 2026 (Pacific Time). Lunch breaks are 12:30 PM Saturday and 12:00 PM Sunday.

Is the festival beginner-friendly?

Entirely. Workshops assume zero experience — "Print your first poster" means it. You'll leave with something you made and at least three new contacts.
